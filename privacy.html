Privacy Policy

VolumeNest is designed to process your audio privately on your Mac.

Information collection

VolumeNest does not collect personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data.

Audio processing

Audio is processed locally and in real time to provide per-application volume control. Audio samples are not recorded, retained, or transmitted over a network.

Application information

VolumeNest reads information about running audio applications so it can display their names and icons. This information remains on your Mac and is not transmitted.

Preferences

Settings such as application volume and mute preferences may be stored locally using macOS system storage. You can remove this data by deleting the app and its local container.

Third parties

VolumeNest contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted on this page. For privacy questions, open a request on the VolumeNest support page.
