Getting started

Ready in three steps.

  1. Open MacMixerLaunch it from Applications. The speaker control appears in the menu bar.
  2. Allow audio accessApprove the macOS audio-capture prompt so MacMixer can process app audio locally.
  3. Build your mixPlay audio, select the output device, then adjust or mute each application.
Requires macOS 14.2 or later Works with built-in, Bluetooth, USB, and HDMI audio devices
Quick help

Common questions

An app is not listed

Make sure the app is open. Start audio playback, wait a moment, and reopen MacMixer from the menu bar.

I cannot hear any sound

Set the app and master controls above 0%, confirm the correct output device is selected, then quit and reopen MacMixer.

Per-app volume does not change

Confirm that MacMixer has audio-capture permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Restart the affected audio app after granting permission.

Bluetooth or USB output changed

Select the new device under Output Device. If it does not appear, reconnect it and reopen MacMixer.

The menu-bar icon is missing

MacMixer uses a compact speaker icon. If the menu bar is full, close an unused menu-bar app or reduce the space occupied by other items, then reopen MacMixer.

How do I reset the mix?

Use Reset to align individual application levels with the master level and clear temporary mute choices.

Does MacMixer record my audio?

No. Audio is processed in real time on your Mac and is not recorded, retained, or transmitted. See the Privacy Policy.
